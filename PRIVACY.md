# SG Bus — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

SG Bus is a home-screen widget that shows live Singapore bus arrival times for the stops you choose.

**What the app uses**
- **Location**, when you tap a widget and during the 15-minute live session that follows, including while the app is
  closed. It is used **only on your phone** to work out which of your saved stops is nearest. Your location is never
  sent anywhere, stored off the device, or shared with anyone.
- **Internet**, to download public bus data from busrouter.sg: arrival times (the request contains only a bus stop
  code) and the list of bus stops. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address to that service.

**What the app does not do:** no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party SDKs, and no data
collection of any kind. Your stops and settings are kept only in the app's own storage on your phone, and are removed
when you uninstall it.

**Data source:** arrival data from arrivelah and busrouter.sg, based on LTA DataMall. SG Bus is independent and is not
affiliated with or endorsed by LTA or the Singapore Government.

**Contact:** via the developer email shown on the app's Google Play page.
