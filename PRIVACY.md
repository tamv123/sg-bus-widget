# SG Bus — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 November 2026_

SG Bus is a home-screen widget that shows live Singapore bus arrival times for the stops you choose.

**What the app uses**
- **Location**, when you tap a widget and during the 15-minute live session that follows, including while the app is
  closed. It is used **only on your phone** to work out which of your saved stops is nearest. Your location is never
  sent anywhere, stored off the device, or shared with anyone.
- **Internet**, to download public bus data from busrouter.sg: arrival times (the request contains only a bus stop
  code) and the list of bus stops. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address to that service.

- **Anonymous usage counts** (on by default; switch off any time in the app under ☰ › Setup). Per day, the app sends:
  how many times widgets were tapped, how many times the app was opened, how many widgets are on the home screen
  (small/large), and how many were added or removed. Each record also carries the app version, whether it is a test
  build, and a random ID the app makes up on first launch. That ID is not linked to you, your phone, your Google
  account or any other app, and it is gone when you uninstall.
  These counts **never include** personal information, your location, your bus stops, or your bus routes and settings.
  They are stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Singapore region), are readable only by the developer, are never shared
  or sold, and are deleted automatically about 13 months after the day they describe. Like any web request, sending
  them reveals your IP address to Google; the app does not store it. Turning the switch off stops counting and
  sending immediately and discards counts not yet sent.

**What the app does not do:** no accounts, no ads, no advertising ID, no tracking across apps or websites, no
third-party SDKs, and nothing sold or shared. Your stops and settings are kept only in the app's own storage on your
phone, and are removed when you uninstall it.

**Data source:** arrival data from arrivelah and busrouter.sg, based on LTA DataMall. SG Bus is independent and is not
affiliated with or endorsed by LTA or the Singapore Government.

**Contact:** via the developer email shown on the app's Google Play page.
